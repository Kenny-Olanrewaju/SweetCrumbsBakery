Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a message below and we'll get back to you.

How far in advance do I need to order a custom cake?

We recommend at least 5–7 days' notice for custom or tiered cakes, and 48 hours for simpler cake orders. Rush orders may be possible depending on our schedule — just reach out and ask.

What areas do you deliver to?

We deliver within a 10-mile radius of downtown Maplewood. Delivery is free on orders over $40; orders below that carry a flat delivery fee, shown at checkout before you confirm payment.

What payment methods do you accept?

You can pay by card at checkout, by bank transfer (we hold your order for 30 minutes while we confirm payment), or in cash/by card directly to the delivery rider.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

Yes, as long as it hasn't gone into production. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number and we'll do our best to accommodate changes or cancellations.

Do you cater to dietary restrictions or allergies?

We offer a small selection of vegan and gluten-free items, and can adapt some recipes on request. Please mention any allergies in your order notes so our bakers can take extra care.

What are your opening hours?

We're open Monday–Saturday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM, and Sunday 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Deliveries are scheduled within these hours.
