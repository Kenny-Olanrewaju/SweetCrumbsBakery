Our Story

Baked With Purpose Since Day One

Sweet Crumbs Bakery began with a simple passion: to share the joy of freshly baked goods with our community. What started as a small home kitchen experiment has blossomed into a beloved Lagos bakery, driven by a commitment to quality ingredients and time-honored baking traditions.

We believe that every bite should be an experience, a moment of comfort and delight. From our artisanal sourdough loaves, crafted with a slow fermentation process for unparalleled flavor, to our decadent pastries and custom-designed cakes, each item is prepared with meticulous care and a sprinkle of love. We source the finest local produce and premium ingredients, ensuring that every creation not only tastes exceptional but also reflects our dedication to wholesome goodness.

Whether you're seeking a daily treat, a special occasion masterpiece, or simply the aroma of warm bread, we invite you to experience the difference at Sweet Crumbs Bakery. We are more than just a bakery; we are a place where delicious memories are made.

“Every loaf tells a story of patience, warmth, and community.” — The Sweet Crumbs Family

The Hands Behind It

Meet Our Bakers

Three bakers, one obsession: getting every loaf, tart, and tier exactly right. Here's who you're really tasting when you order from us.

Amara Adeyemi

Founder & Head Baker

Amara started Sweet Crumbs in her home kitchen 10 years ago, obsessing over slow-fermented sourdough until the neighbors started placing orders. She still shapes the day's first loaves herself.

“Good bread doesn't rush — and neither do we.”

Chidi Okafor

Executive Pastry Chef

Classically trained with a weakness for laminated dough, Chidi runs our pastry program — croissants, tarts, and éclairs built layer by patient layer, every single morning.

“Butter, time, and patience — that's the whole secret.”

Zainab Bello

Cake Artist & Decorator

Zainab designs every custom and wedding cake we send out, from hand-piped sugar flowers to hand-painted finishes. She still gets nervous before every reveal.

“Every cake should tell the story of the day it's made for.”
